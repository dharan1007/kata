# Code of Conduct

KATA is an open engineering and research-automation community. Participation is welcome regardless of experience level, background or employer, provided discussion remains technically rigorous and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, code and evidence rather than people.
- Distinguish verified behavior from assumptions and proposed work.
- Give reproducible reasoning when disagreeing on protocol or connector behavior.
- Help first-time contributors reduce problems into testable cases.
- Respect privacy and credentials.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when recommending APIs, vendors or benchmarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, fabricated connector/benchmark evidence, deceptive contribution activity and attempts to manipulate stars, votes or contributor counts are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Security-sensitive reports must follow `SECURITY.md` instead of being posted publicly. For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile and include enough context to investigate without reposting private information publicly.
